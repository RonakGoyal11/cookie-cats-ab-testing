# Initial SQL Findings

## Experiment

The control group experienced the progression gate at level 30, while the
treatment group experienced the gate at level 40.

## Sample Allocation

- Control, gate_30: 44,700 players
- Treatment, gate_40: 45,489 players

## Retention Results

- Day 1 retention decreased from 44.82% to 44.23%.
- Absolute Day 1 effect: -0.59 percentage points.
- Relative Day 1 effect: -1.32%.

- Day 7 retention decreased from 19.02% to 18.20%.
- Absolute Day 7 effect: -0.82 percentage points.
- Relative Day 7 effect: -4.31%.

## Engagement Results

- Average rounds excluding the extreme outlier were 51.34 for gate_30 and
  51.30 for gate_40.
- The mean difference was only -0.04 rounds.
- Median rounds decreased from 17 to 16.

## Preliminary Interpretation

Moving the progression gate from level 30 to level 40 does not appear to
improve player behaviour. Retention was lower in the treatment group, while
average engagement remained nearly unchanged.

Statistical testing is required before making a final rollout decision.
